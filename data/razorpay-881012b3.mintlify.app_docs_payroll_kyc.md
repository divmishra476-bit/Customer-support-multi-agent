# KYC Verification

> Verify KYC and activate your RazorpayX Payroll account. Refer to the process and requirements.

After you sign up with [Payroll](https://payroll.razorpay.com/dashboard), start the KYC verification process to use all the Payroll [features](/docs/payroll).

## How it Works

There are two steps to verify KYC:

1. Verify bank account details.
2. Upload documents of:
   1. [Authorised signatory](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/glossary/#:~:text=days%20of%20creation.-,Authorised%20Signatory,-A%20person%20who).
   2. Company's details using [applicable documents](#kyc-documents).

## Best Practices

* You can complete your account activation process in any preferred order. We auto-save your application.
* Ensure all document copies uploaded are:
  * Less than 5 MB in size.
  * Not password protected.
  * Not blurry and unclear.
  * In `pdf`, `jpg`, `png` formats.
* Proof of identity (PAN) and [authorised signatory's](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/glossary/#:~:text=days%20of%20creation.-,Authorised%20Signatory,-A%20person%20who) address proof (Aadhar Card and Passport) should belong to the same person.
* Do not close the window when verification is in progress.
* When providing company details:
  * If you are an existing [Razorpay PG](/docs/payments) merchant, the KYC fields are auto-filled and verified.
  * If you are an existing [RazorpayX](/docs/x) user or you are using Razorpay products for the first time, follow the [below steps](#get-started) to verify your KYC.

## Verify KYC

To initiate KYC and activate your Payroll account:

1. Log in to the [Payroll Dashboard](https://payroll.razorpay.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Start** in the Organisation Setup widget on the Home Page.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/xpayroll-kyc-setup.jpg" alt="KYC onboarding page on Payroll Dashboard" width="500" />
3. Upload your bank details like account number and IFSC. You can wait for the details to be automatically verified by the system or complete the KYC verification process manually.
4. Click **Verify**. You can proceed to provide KYC documents for company details.
5. Provide the following details:
   1. **Address Proof**: Upload your business address proof.
      1. Select an address proof document from the drop-down list.
      2. Upload your address proof document's front and back sides as applicable.
   2. **Identity Proof**: Upload your PAN card copy for identity proof verification. The authorised signatory's address proof and the personal PAN card must match.
   3. **Business Documents**: Upload the relevant [business documents](#kyc-documents) based on your business type.

      <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/xpayroll-kyc-upload-docs.jpg" alt="Upload relevant documents for Payroll KYC Verification" width="500" />
6. Click **Verify** after uploading all the documents. The KYC verification process takes 1-2 working days.
