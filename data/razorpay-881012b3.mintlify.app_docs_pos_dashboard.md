# About Dashboard

> Introduction to the Razorpay POS Dashboard, prerequisites and various Razorpay Dashboard actions.

Dashboard offers a wide range of features, such as:

* **Analytics** and **real-time charts** that provide valuable insight into your business performance.
* List of **recent activities** that may require your action.
* **Configure, operate and manage** your POS account and other Razorpay products.
* An overview of recent **payments, settlements and refunds**.

### Prerequisites

You must have a Razorpay account to access the Dashboard. Do not have a Razorpay account? [Sign up with Razorpay](/docs/pos/quickstart).
