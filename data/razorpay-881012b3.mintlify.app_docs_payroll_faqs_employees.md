## Employee FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I change my tax regime?">
    You can request your manager to [make changes to your tax regime](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#regime-changes). Otherwise, you can change when filing your Income Tax Returns (ITR) in July.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I declare or modify proofs uploaded on the Payroll Dashboard?">
    You can declare, modify and upload new proofs only when the Proof Upload window is open. Know how to [upload investment proofs](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#declare-upload-proofs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I save tax?">
    Know how you can [reduce your tax liability](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#reduce-tax-liability).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my proof rejected?">
    Your proofs may be rejected due to incorrect or insufficient information to validate your declarations. Know how to [resolve failed declarations](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#resolve-failed-declarations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I apply for reimbursements?">
    Follow the steps to [apply and claim reimbursements](/docs/payroll/employees/reimbursements).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am an employee and I have received an email that Payroll has changed/removed by tax declaration. Why is that?">
    You receive such email when either your proofs do not match your declaration, or the proof uploaded has incorrect information. Know how to [resolve failed declarations](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#resolve-failed-declarations).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the TDS deducted from my salary higher than the previous month, due to which my in-hand salary is also less?">
    Your in-hand salary is less due to a higher TDS deduction applicable on:

    * A bonus payout you received. <br />

      Suppose we do not deduct the increased TDS for a bonus in the current month. In that case, employees must pay the TDS shortfall in the following months. As a result, the in-hand salary also reduces for the following months even if the bonus was only for the current month.

    * Proofs verified and approved.  <br />

      Higher TDS deductions happen on the basis of the proofs approved by your organisation. Ensure you upload proofs that match your declaration amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am able to view my declared and approved tax deductions on the Tax Deductions page. However, they are not visible on my payslip. Why?">
    If you are unable to view your deductions on your payslip, it is because you have not selected your preferred tax regime. [Select tax regime](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#select-regime) and confirm selection.

    Your deductions may also not appear on your payslip if you have chosen the new tax regime.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My TDS deduction is higher in the current month than my previous months. Why?">
    If you do not submit proofs or have submitted proofs lesser than the declared amount, the TDS deduction is higher. Ensure you [submit investment proofs](/docs/payroll/employees/declarations#declare-upload-proofs) before the proof upload window closes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the PF deductions from my salary considered for tax exemptions?">
    Yes, PF deductions from your salary are considered for tax exemptions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I download Form 12BB from?">
    Your employers can provide the signed Form 12BB. Form 12BB provides a list of declarations you have provided to your organisation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my organisation-provided medical insurance exempt from tax?">
    Yes, the medical insurance provided by your organisation is exempt under Section 80D to a certain limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My current monthly rent is shown 0 even after declaring the HRA. Why?">
    Your current monthly rent may be 0 as the HRA declared is applicable for a different duration such as April 2024 to August 2024. Ensure you declare the HRA for the current month/quarter.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My CTC has been revised but I cannot see the updated amount. Why?">
    You can view your revised CTC only after the payroll for the revision is executed. Until then, you continue to see the CTC used in your last executed payroll.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
