## Contact Razorpay Support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="To contact the Razorpay Support team:">
    1. Log in to the Dashboard.

    2. Click **Help & Support**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-support.jpg" width="800" alt="Raise a Ticket - Help and Support pop-up - Razorpay Dashboard" />

    3. Check the FAQs for common concerns. If the FAQs can not resolve your concern, click **Contact Us**.

    4. You have the following options in the **Contact Us** section:
       * [Create ticket](#create-support-ticket): Check existing queries or raise a new support ticket.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/dashboard-support-options-new.jpg" width="250" alt="Raise a Ticket - Help and Support pop-up - Razorpay Dashboard" />
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Create Support Ticket

You can check existing queries or raise a new support ticket through the Dashboard.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="To raise a new ticket:">
    1. Log in to the Dashboard.
    2. Navigate to **Account & Settings** → **Support tickets** (under Business settings).
    3. Click **Support tickets**.
    4. Click **+ Raise New Query** and select the relevant topic and sub-topic.
    5. Enter the details of your query and **Upload file** if necessary. Click **Create support ticket**.
    6. **Confirm** your phone number. The support ticket is created and details are sent to your registered email id. Click **Done**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
