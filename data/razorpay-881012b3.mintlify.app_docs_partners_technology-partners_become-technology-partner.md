# Become a Technology Partner

> Submit a request to become a Razorpay Technology Partner to offer a seamless payment experience for your clients within your website/app.

All Partners are onboarded as Service Partners by default. You can request a switch if you have a use case for the Technology Partner type. Know how to become a [Razorpay Partner](/docs/partners/new-to-razorpay).

## Request a Switch

To request a Partner type switch:

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.
3. Click **Explore Now**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-explore-now.jpg" alt="Initiate Partner type switch" width="800" />
4. On the **Tell us more about the services you provide** page, select the relevant business type and click **Next**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-services-offered.jpg" alt="Partner type switch - What services do you offer?" width="800" />
5. If your business type is best suited for the Service Partner type, you will see the below screen.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-denied.jpg" alt="Partner type switch denied" width="800" />
6. If a different Partner type is more suitable for your business, you will see the below screen.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-evaluate-usecase.jpg" alt="Partner type switch - evaluate business use case" width="800" />
7. Select **Yes** if you have a product to manage payments for your sub-merchants and click **Next**.
8. Enter your phone number, website URL, and business details, and click **Next**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-type-switch-business-details.jpg" alt="Partner type switch - fill in details" width="800" />

You have successfully submitted a request for a Partner type switch. Our team will reach out to you with further instructions.

## Next Steps

If your switch request is approved, you will have to complete [full KYC](/docs/partners/technology-partners/kyc) to start receiving commissions.

<Warning>
  **Watch Out!**

  Filling out the form does not guarantee a Partner type switch. Our team will evaluate your use case and contact you for more information. This process can take around 2 to 3 weeks.
</Warning>
