# Banner in Bills

> Customise your digital bills with banners, announcements, limited-time offers or product highlights, to boost engagement and retention.

Add eye-catching banners to the top of your digital bills to promote sales, announce special offers or showcase new services. Upload your own images or choose from ready-made templates, include clickable links and set when your banners appear. It's an easy way to reach customers and grow your business right from your bills.

## Create New Banner In Bill Campaign

To create a new Banner in Bill Campaign:

1. Click **Banner in Bill** under **Digital Bills**. The **Banner In Bill** page appears with the list of all banner in bills campaigns.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/banner-in-bill-page.jpg" alt="Banner In Bills Page" width="800" />
2. Click **+ New Banner In Bill Campaign**. The **Banner in Bill - New Campaign** page appears.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/create-banner-in-bill.jpg" alt="Create Banner In Bills" width="800" />
3. Enter the **Campaign Details**, select company and click **Next Step**. The **Banner Builder** page appears.
   * You can add banner components such as images, Carousels or GIFs.
   * You can choose media directly from your recent uploads.
   * You can also add any coupons on the banner.

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/banner-builder-banner-in-bill.jpg" alt="Build Your Banner In Bills" width="800" />

4. Select an image for more options such as adding redirect url, survey url or custom url. Click **Next Step**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/banner-builder-embed-urls.jpg" alt="Embed URLs in Your Banner In Bills" width="800" />
5. On the **Campaign Settings** page, select your **Brand** and **Stores**. You can also choose to publish the campaign immediately or schedule it. Click **Next Step**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/campaign-settings-banner-in-bill.jpg" alt="Campaign Settings in Banner In Bill" width="800" />
6. On the **Preview & Publish** page you can check all campaign details. Click **Export Report** and enter your email to opt for exporting campaign reports. Click **Publish**.
   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/preview-pane-banner-in-bills.jpg" alt="Preview and Publish Banner In Bill" width="800" />

Your **Banner In Bill** Campaign is published and appears in the list of campaigns.
