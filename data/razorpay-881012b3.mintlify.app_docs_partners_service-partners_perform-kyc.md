# Perform Client KYC

> Know how to perform KYC for your clients from the Partner Dashboard.

Offer a smooth onboarding experience by assisting your client with their KYC.
You can request KYC access from your client:

* [When sending an invite](#request-kyc-access-when-sending-an-invite)
* [During client onboarding](#request-kyc-access-during-client-onboarding)

After the client approves your request, you can [perform their KYC](#perform-kyc).

#### Advantages

* **Decreases Onboarding Time by 50%** <br />
  Partner performing KYC for sub-merchants decreases onboarding time by half and ensures a seamless onboarding experience.
* **Value Added Service** <br />
  Partner performing KYC acts as a delight feature for your clients.

## Request KYC Access When Sending an Invite

You can request to perform KYC for your client when inviting them. During onboarding, your client will see a consent screen to allow you the access to perform KYC. They need to approve it for you to submit the KYC form on their behalf.

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/onboarding-kyc-consent-screen.jpg" alt="KYC consent form during client onboarding." width="250" />

### Invite Using Email

When inviting a client using email, you can opt in to assist their KYC by selecting **Yes, I will assist my client with their KYC** in the **Assist the client with their KYC screen**.

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-add-sumb-do-kyc-bulk.jpg" alt="Service Partners - perform client KYC" width="250" />

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="To request KYC access when sending an invite using an email:">
    1. Log in to the Dashboard.
    2. Click **Partner** and navigate to **Affiliate Accounts**.
    3. Select the **Payments** or **RazorpayX** tab depending on the product you want to refer your affiliate.
    4. Click **+ Add New Clients**.
           <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-reseller-add-client.jpg" alt="Service Partners - add new clients" width="800" />
    5. Under the **Using Email** tab, enter the **Client Name**, **Email ID** and **Contact number** (*optional*) to which the invite link must be sent. Click **Next**.
           <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-add_sub_merchant.jpg" alt="Service Partners - enter client details" width="250" />
    6. Select **Yes, I will assist my client with their KYC** and click **Send Invite**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Invite using Referral Link

When inviting a client using referral link, you can opt-in to assist their KYC by selecting **Yes, I will assist my client with their KYC**. This will generate a new link initiating a request for KYC access from the client during the onboarding process

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/referral-link-with-kyc.jpg" alt="Service Partners - share referral link with KYC" width="250" />

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="To request KYC access when inviting using referral link:">
    1. Log in to the Dashboard.
    2. In the **Partner** section, navigate to **Affiliate Accounts**.
    3. Click **Share Referral Link**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-share-referral-link.jpg" alt="Service Partners - share referral link" width="800" />
    4. For Partner accounts on which X referrals have been activated in the **Share Referral Link** modal, select Razorpay Payments or RazorpayX depending on the product you want to refer an affiliate.
    5. Select **Yes, I will assist my client with their KYC**.
    6. A drop-down page appears where you can share the link using:
       * Copy button: This copies the referral link. You can copy-paste this link and send it via email or instant messaging apps such as WhatsApp.
       * Facebook: Click the Facebook icon to share the referral link as a post on your Facebook account.
       * Twitter: Click the Twitter icon to send a tweet with the referral link.
       * WhatsApp: Click the WhatsApp icon to send a message with the referral link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Request KYC Access During Client Onboarding

You can request and re-request KYC access during your client's onboarding journey.

<Warning>
  **Watch Out**

  You can only raise 3 requests per client for completing the KYC on their behalf. If the client declines all the 3 requests, you cannot send any more requests.
</Warning>

To request KYC access:

1. In the **Affiliate Accounts** section of your Dashboard, click **Request for KYC** under the **Actions** column for the client you want to perform KYC.
   <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-dashboard-affiliates.jpg" alt="request kyc" width="800" />
2. After you click on **Request for KYC**, a side panel opens. Click **Request for KYC access** to raise a request. You can also find link to documentation for documents required for KYC in the side panel.
   <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/request-kyc.jpg" alt="request kyc access" width="300" />

## Perform KYC

After you request for KYC access:

1. The client receives an email to approve or reject the KYC request.
   <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/x-partnerships-approval-email.jpg" alt="approval email" width="300" />
2. If the client approves the request, you can fill in the KYC details from the Partner Dashboard by clicking on **Perform KYC**. You can check the [KYC access request status](#check-kyc-access-request-status) on the Partner Dashboard.
   <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/easy-submit.jpg" alt="Business Details - Submit KYC Details" width="800" />
3. You can view the activation statuses as shown here:
   <img class="click-zoom" src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-activation-status.jpg" alt="activation status" width="800" />

## Check KYC Access Request Status

You can view the status of request on the Partner Dashboard under the **Actions** tab. Refer to the table below for all statuses and their description.

| Action                              | Description                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Request Merchant for KYC access     | Approval required to view Merchant KYC                                                                                                                                                   |
| Request approved by Merchant        | Merchant has approved the KYC access request                                                                                                                                             |
| Request expired                     | New KYC access request needs to be initiated by Partner                                                                                                                                  |
| Waiting for Merchant approval       | Merchant approval for KYC access is pending                                                                                                                                              |
| Request rejected by Merchant once   | Merchant has rejected your KYC access request                                                                                                                                            |
| Request rejected by Merchant twice  | Merchant has rejected your KYC access request 2 times. You can request for access only one more time, if the merchant still doesn't approve, you wont be able to send the request again. |
| Request rejected by Merchant thrice | Merchant has rejected your KYC access request multiple times. Now, only the merchant can perform their KYC through their Dashboard.                                                      |
