The following table records changes made to RazorpayX Payroll since Jan 2024:

| Month-Year | Feature                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| May 2026   | Flexible Benefits          | Introduced the [Flexi Component Library](/docs/payroll/component-library/flexible-benefits) to define and manage Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP) components such as Meal Allowance, Car Lease Reimbursement and Employer NPS Contribution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| April 2026 | Multiple Salary Structures | Create and manage [multiple salary structures](/docs/payroll/multiple-salary-structure) across employee groups, along with new [Compensation](/docs/payroll/compensation) pages for CTC components, Flexi components, Perquisites and Recurring Deductions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| April 2026 | Tax Codes                  | Map TDS codes to salary components using the [Tax Codes](/docs/payroll/tax-verifications/tax-codes) setup for accurate tax filings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Feb 2026   | Salary Component Library   | Expanded the [Salary Component Library](/docs/payroll/component-library) with Deduction, Non-Payable Benefits and Perquisites categories, alongside Earnings. 18 predefined deduction components are now available.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Dec 2025   | Multi-location ESI         | Set up, pay and check [ESI compliance payments across multiple locations](/docs/payroll/multi-location-esi).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| April 2025 | Form 24Q                   | Payroll introduced check for  [accurate 24Q filing.](/docs/payroll/tds/form-24Q)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| March 2025 | Salary Component Library   | Create [custom salary components](/docs/payroll/component-library) for your organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Feb 2025   | Provident Fund             | Payroll has resumed automated registration and PF payments for employees. This was paused due to 2FA rule on the PF portal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Dec 2024   | Tax Deductions             | Create [investment declaration and proof upload windows](/docs/payroll/tax-deductions-setup) for your organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Nov 2024   | 2FA                        | Introduced [2-factor authentication](/docs/payroll/administrator#two-factor-authentication) for the following actions: <br /> <ul><li><a href="/docs/payroll/payroll-payouts#contractor-payments" target="_blank">Contractor Payments</a></li><li><a href="/docs/payroll/user-roles-workflows#create-user-roles" target="_blank">User role creation</a></li><li><a href="/docs/payroll/run-payroll#execute-payroll" target="_blank">Payroll execution</a></li><li><a href="/docs/payroll/advance-salary#pay-advance-salary" target="_blank">Pay advance salary</a></li><li><a href="/docs/payroll/reimbursements#pay-reimbursements" target="_blank">Pay reimbursements</a></li><li><a href="/docs/payroll/loans#create-a-loan" target="_blank">Create an employee loan</a></li><li>API access</li> </ul> <br /> |
| Sep 2024   | Professional Tax           | Due to mandatory 2FA implementation by the Karnataka Government, [automated PT payments](/docs/payroll/professional-tax#pt-payment-for-karnataka-employees) via Payroll for employees residing in Karnataka are temporarily paused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Aug 2024   | Provident Fund (PF)        | Due to mandatory 2FA implemented by the EPFO, [automated PF payments and registration](/docs/payroll/faqs#pf-payments) for employees via the Payroll Dashboard is temporarily paused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Aug 2024   | 2FA                        | Introduced mandatory [2-factor authentication](/docs/payroll/administrator#two-factor-authentication) for administrators and user roles during login when accessing the Payroll Dashboard for secure money movement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Aug 2024   | Budget Changes             | Revised the payroll calculations per the [changes introduced](/docs/payroll/run-payroll) in the Union Budget for the new regime.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| July 2024  | LWF                        | Introduced [enabling LWF for employees](/docs/payroll/statutory-compliance#labour-welfare-fund-lwf) from their profiles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| May 2024   | Fund Loading               | Introduced Source Account Validation to ensure secure fund transfer and payments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| May 2024   | Approval Workflow          | You can set up [approval workflow](/docs/payroll/approval-workflow) and add approvers for core payroll and salary revision actions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
