RazorpayX Payroll supports two ways to process your monthly payroll:

* Via Payroll Wallet
* Via RazorpayX-powered Current Account

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/current-account-x.jpg" width="800" />

## What's the Difference?

This section highlights the key differences in features, benefits and applications of each offering, enabling you to make an informed decision tailored to your circumstances.

| **Feature**                                                                               | **RazorpayX Payroll via Wallet**                                                                 | **RazorpayX Payroll via Linked-Current Account**                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core Payroll Features: Salary calculations, disbursals, Compliance payments & Filings** | ✓                                                                                                | ✓                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Full Stack Contractor Payments & Filing: Part-time employee & Vendor Payments**         | <ul><li>Only Part-time Employee payments are supported</li><li>NO automated 26Q filing</li></ul> | <ul><li>Part-time Employee Payments</li><li>Vendor Payments</li><li>Auto 26Q Filing</li></ul>                                                                                                                          |
| **Frictionless money movement**                                                           | <ul><li> Manual fund loading every month</li></ul>                                               | <ul><li>No manual fund loading(as payments are directly triggered via Current Account)</li><li>Faster Salary Disbursals</li></ul>                                                                                      |
| **Exclusive features**                                                                    | -                                                                                                | <ul>Access exclusive features like:<li>Bank Account Verification of all your Employees.</li><li>Ensure salary is getting credited to the right Employee</li></ul>                                                      |
| **Access to RazorpayX Business Banking+ Platform**                                        | ✗                                                                                                | <ul><li>Get access to our Business Banking+ dashboard for all your other Payouts like Refunds, Cashbacks, Incentives, Loans, Claims, Winnings and so on.</li><li>Get access to APIs, Bulk and Single Payouts</li></ul> |
