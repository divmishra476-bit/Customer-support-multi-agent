### Sign Up and KYC

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sign up on  Partners Page">
    If you are new to Razorpay, you must first sign up for a Razorpay account.

    Follow these steps:

    1. Navigate to the [Partners](https://razorpay.com/partners) webpage and click **Become a Partner** or **Sign Up**.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Sign up using email ID">
        2) Enter your email id and click **Continue**.
        3) Enter a password and click **Create Password**.
                   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-password.jpg" alt="partners - enter password" width="800" />
        4) Enter the OTP sent to your registered email and click **Verify**.
        5) Enter your name and optionally, your number. Click **Continue**.
                   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-name.jpg" alt="partners - enter name" width="800" />

        This completes the account creation process.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Sign up using phone number">
        2. Enter your phone number and click **Continue**.
        3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered phone number and click **Verify**.
                   <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-onboarding-new-otp.jpg" alt="partners - enter otp" width="800" />
        4. Enter your name and optionally, your email. Click **Continue**.

        This completes the account creation process.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Complete KYC on  Dashboard">
    As a Razorpay Partner, you are eligible to receive [commissions](/docs/partners/commissions) from Razorpay. You must complete the account activation and KYC process to receive the commission. We recommend you perform this step using a desktop computer.

    To activate your account:

    1. Log in to the Dashboard and navigate to **Partner** → **Home**.
    2. Click **Submit KYC**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partners-dashboard-submit-kyc.jpg" alt="Complete Partner KYC on Razorpay Dashboard" width="800" />
    3. In the **CONTACT DETAILS** section, fill in your name, email and number. Click **Save & Next**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-contact-details.jpg" alt="Partners KYC Contact Details" width="800" />
    4. In the **BUSINESS DETAILS** section, select your **Business Type** and fill relevant details. Click **Save & Next**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-business-details.jpg" alt="Partners KYC Business Details" width="800" />
    5. Fill the **ADDRESS DETAILS** section. Click **Save**.
           <img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/partner-kyc-address-details.jpg" alt="Partners KYC Address Details" width="800" />
    6. Read and agree to the **Razorpay Terms and Conditions**. Click **Submit and Verify**.

    After you submit the details, our team will review the KYC form.

    * We will notify you via email, SMS and WhatsApp if we need further information.
    * The review process takes 4-5 days.
    * Upon successful verification:
      * Razorpay displays the **Settlements Enabled** success message on the Dashboard.
      * Razorpay deposits the settlement amount in your bank account per your settlement schedule.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
