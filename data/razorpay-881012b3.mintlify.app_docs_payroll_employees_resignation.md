# Resignation

> Use the RazorpayX Payroll Dashboard as an employee for resignation.

You can submit your resignation to your organisation on the Payroll Dashboard. After you submit a resignation request, your manager/administrator may approve it, post which we settle your full and final settlement (FNF).

## Submit Resignation

1. Log in to the [Payroll Dashboard](https://payroll.razorpay.com/dashboard)
2. Navigate to the user icon on the top right corner and click **My profile**.
3. On **User Profile** page, click **SUBMIT RESIGNATION** at the bottom of your profile. This opens the **Employee Resignation** page.
   1. Review the basic information such as the **Name**, **Email** and **Manager**.
   2. Provide the resignation details such as the **Date of Resignation**, **Reason for Resignation** and other **Comments**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the Date of Resignation the same as the Last Working Day?">
    No, they are not the same.

    * **Date of Resignation**: Marks the date when your notice period begins.
    * **Last Working Day**: Marks the end of your notice period, after which you you are relieved from your duties in the organisation.

    Consider the following example:

    | Term                 | Date/Duration     |
    | -------------------- | ----------------- |
    | Date of Resignation  | August 30         |
    | Notice Period Policy | One month/30 days |

    In this case, your Last Working Day (LWD) will be September 30, post which we process your FNF.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

1. Click **SUBMIT RESIGNATION**.

<img src="https://razorpay.com/docs/build/browser/assets/images/payroll-employees-resignation.jpg" alt="Payroll Dashboard Employee submit resignation" width="800" />

You have successfully submitted a resignation request to your manager. We notify you about your resignation status via email. You can also check your status in the **My Profile** → **Resignation Status** section.
