Mini POS devices are ultra-compact payment acceptance solutions designed to work with smartphones and tablets. These pocket-sized terminals enable businesses to transform mobile devices into complete payment systems, offering maximum portability and flexibility for merchants of all sizes.

## How Mini POS Works

Mini POS solutions operate through a simple but effective system:

1. **Hardware Connection**: The Mini POS device connects to a smartphone or tablet via Bluetooth
2. **Software Integration**: A companion application on the mobile device communicates with the Mini POS terminal
3. **Transaction Processing**: Payments are processed through the mobile device's internet connection
4. **Receipt Delivery**: Digital receipts can be sent via email or SMS, or printed with optional mobile printers
